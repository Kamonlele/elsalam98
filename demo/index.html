The most serious maritime disaster in Egypt’s history.

Why this site exists

We are the relatives of the passengers who died. We created this site to bring the facts to light and to reach the truth.

Exposing the mistakes made on that ship can help prevent them from happening again, and make navigation safer for everyone.

Since 2010, more than 15% of the families affected, together with dozens of survivors and crew members, have been taking part in the legal case in Italy. It is the largest shipwreck lawsuit currently under way in the world.

One ship, rebuilt into another

In 1991 four decks were added to the Boccaccio. She came out taller, heavier and less stable. Two of the five sister ships transformed this way sank within four years.
